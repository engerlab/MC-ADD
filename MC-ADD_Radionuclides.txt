:::                MC-ADD: Radionuclides available in the GUI           :::
:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

The following radionuclides can be selected from the "Radioactive source"
drop-down menu of the MC-ADD GUI. Their decay is simulated by the Geant4
Radioactive Decay Module, using the nuclear data distributed with Geant4.
Some radionuclides appear in more than one category, as they emit more than
one type of radiation.

Other particle sources (e.g., monoenergetic beams) can be defined by
modifying the generated macro file, as described in the README.


::: Alpha emitters :::
    Am-241    Ra-224    Pu-239    Ra-226    Ac-225
    Th-232    Th-228    U-238     U-235     At-211
    Po-210    Cm-244    Cf-252    Rn-222

    Ir-192    Fe-59     Cs-137    Co-60     Na-24
